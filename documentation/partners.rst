Partners
========

CLAMS is developed by the `Brandeis Lab for Linguistics and Computation (LLC) <https://brandeis-llc.github.io/>`_ in close partnership with the GBH Archives, home of the American Archive of Public Broadcasting, and is funded by the Mellon Foundation.

GBH and the American Archive of Public Broadcasting
---------------------------------------------------

`GBH <https://www.wgbh.org/>`_ is the Boston public media producer that, together with the Library of Congress, stewards the `American Archive of Public Broadcasting (AAPB) <https://americanarchive.org/>`_. The AAPB preserves and provides access to historically significant public radio and television programs from stations and producers across the United States.

The GBH Archives has been the CLAMS project's primary partner since the project began in 2017. GBH archivists define the cataloging problems that CLAMS apps address, contribute training and evaluation data drawn from AAPB holdings, and put the resulting tools to work in their cataloging workflows. Their feedback drives what gets built and how it is evaluated. For an archivist's view of this collaboration, see the AAPB blog posts on `AI-assisted audiovisual cataloging <https://blog.americanarchive.org/2026/04/21/outside-the-black-box-ai-shedding-light-for-av-cataloging/>`_ and on `implementing language models at GBH <https://blog.americanarchive.org/2026/04/21/connecting-with-the-past-by-connecting-with-others/>`_.

Mellon Foundation
-----------------

The `Mellon Foundation <https://www.mellon.org/>`_ has supported the AAPB through a series of grants to GBH, and Brandeis has taken part in each of them as the developer of CLAMS.

* 2017: a two-year, $1 million grant to `expand the AAPB's intake capacity and collaborative infrastructure <https://americanarchive.org/about-the-american-archive/projects/mellon>`_, during which the LLC began building the CLAMS platform.
* 2019: a two-year, $750,000 grant, `From Repository to Resource <https://americanarchive.org/about-the-american-archive/projects/mellon2>`_, which paired the AAPB with the LLC to use machine learning to capture richer metadata from radio and television programs.
* 2022: a four-year, `$16 million grant <https://www.mellon.org/grant-details/american-archive-of-public-broadcasting-20452962>`_ to preserve endangered public media holdings at scale. Brandeis received an `$825,000 subgrant <https://www.brandeis.edu/now/2023/february/pbs-film-archive-computational-linguitics.html>`_ to apply CLAMS to a substantially larger and more diverse collection and to develop workflows that let archivists adapt current AI tools to new material.
